GROUP EXHIBITION

La Partie

Alexandre Yang · Irène Eon · Manuel Aparicio Ugalde · Timothée Gruel

16 March — 10 April 2024

Curated by Mathilde Badie

Exhibition

At the outset, there is an obsession with painting and a desire to capture those we love. Familiar faces, exchanged glances, affinities: over years of parallel practices and reciprocal influences, lines of convergence have emerged between the works of Alexandre Yang, Irène Eon, Manuel Aparicio Ugalde and Timothée Gruel.

Animal presences, legends and costumes are mixed with gentle figures and domestic scenes; echoes and shifts in scale appear like visual winks. Within this shared energy, tones begin to resonate and silhouettes seem to find their reflections.

What unfolds here is perhaps the birth of a common mythology — one in which the spectacular is located within the intimate, the everyday and the familiar. Painting leaves room for play, chance and coincidence.

My first is a piece of a whole,
My second gathers the players,
My third is the movement of a melody,
My fourth extends like a celebration,
My whole is a game.

Curated and written by Mathilde Badie
