ARTISTS EXHIBITIONS ABOUT CONTACT

SOLO EXHIBITION

Une insomnie m’attend, large comme les astres

Haeji Lim

19 May — 22 June 2023

Exhibition

Everyday life continues with countless worries that remain unresolved from morning until night, making sleep difficult. At times, the confusion produced by these concerns makes it difficult to distinguish between what belongs to our real life and what may belong to an imagined or false one.

Haeji Lim’s work seeks to give form to uncomfortable moments and to the large and small emotions that remain engraved within her. Her abstract images emerge from the space between the inner self and the external world.

The absence of unhappiness does not necessarily mean that life consists only of happy days. Fatigue, sadness and anger can coexist with the apparent continuity of everyday life, which carries on even when nothing seems to change.

Yet Lim does not allow these emotions to remain entirely within darkness. She speaks of creating a “white heart” for the future. On her canvases, light and darkness therefore intermingle, producing grey stories in which opposing emotional states exist together.

Her works depict everyday life through a predominantly monochrome language. What appears may belong to the past, the present or the future, without these temporal dimensions being clearly separated from one another.

Sleepless nights and unresolved concerns continue, but the act of painting leaves traces. Through these traces, emotions, anxieties and lived experiences can be shared. Ultimately, Lim hopes that this recognition of shared vulnerability might allow people to encourage one another.
