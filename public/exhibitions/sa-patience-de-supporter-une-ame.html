GROUP EXHIBITION

Sa patience de supporter une âme

4 July — 12 September 2026

Exhibition

The title Sa patience de supporter une âme is drawn from Rainer Maria Rilke's The Notebooks of Malte Laurids Brigge.

The exhibition brings together works that explore different ways in which an image can hold, suggest or sustain an inner presence. Rather than defining the soul as a fixed religious or psychological idea, the exhibition approaches it as a way of looking — a space in which images, forms and materials remain open to memory, perception and interior experience.
