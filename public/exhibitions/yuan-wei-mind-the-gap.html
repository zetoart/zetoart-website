SOLO EXHIBITION

Mind the Gap

Yuan Wei

18 May — 15 June 2024

Exhibition

Mind the Gap is Yuan Wei’s first solo exhibition in Paris, bringing together works from the past decade.

“Corners” emerge as an important theme and form throughout the exhibition. As spaces of imbalance, corners are at once particular and ubiquitous. They lack complete spatial stability, resist strict geometric order and often appear in an almost uncontrolled state.

Yuan Wei is particularly interested in the relationship between order and chaos. A corner belongs fully to neither: it occupies an unstable space between the two. Within these corners, subtle gaps seem to arise rather than being deliberately arranged. These fissures make disorder possible, allowing imperfection and chaos to enter seemingly stable structures.

The artist relates this shift to changes within her own life. Earlier years were structured around study and work, while later experiences of teaching, family and parenthood introduced greater complexity. Her attention consequently moved toward imbalanced relationships, unstable structures and states of disorder.

Beneath apparently stable surfaces, unpredictable forms of chaos remain hidden. Yuan Wei’s paintings encourage us to change our position and look from multiple angles, suggesting that another perspective may reveal a different understanding of the same space.

Another dimension of the exhibition appears in the Alchemy series. Children’s building blocks and the gaps between them become metaphors for human relationships. Differences of position and distance create different relational structures, while simple geometries and words map the invisible spaces that exist between individuals.

A gap may be a fissure, a distance or a difference. Whether understood physically or socially, it reveals the presence of a boundary. Yuan Wei’s works continually draw attention to these boundaries, echoing the familiar announcement of the London Underground: “Mind the gap.”
