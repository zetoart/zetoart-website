GROUP EXHIBITION

Parasite

Bogdan Đukanović · Capucine Merle · Pauline Simon · Thomas Besset · Libo Wei

28 June — 13 July 2024

Exhibition

Parasite explores the symbiosis and antagonism of materials through the prism of contemporary art. Five young artists bring together a wide range of techniques and media to create works that question the nature of coexistence.

Through encounters between marble, 3D printing, ceramics, wood, metal, resin and even food, the exhibition moves beyond conventional boundaries between materials and considers the parasite as both a biological condition and an artistic metaphor.

In biology, a parasite is an organism that lives at the expense of another, often within a complex relationship of dependence and conflict. Transposed into artistic practice, this notion becomes a way of examining how different materials interact. Rather than simply being juxtaposed, they merge, resist one another and are forced to coexist in unexpected ways.

Marble, traditionally associated with permanence and solidity, encounters materials defined by decay and transformation. Metal, cold and industrial, meets the flexibility of contemporary fabrication techniques. Fragile ceramics enter into dialogue with the warmth and robustness of wood, while resin occupies an ambiguous position between the organic and the artificial, capable of both preserving and transforming what it contains.

Each work thus becomes a microcosm in which opposing forces confront and accommodate one another. These tensions allow the artists to challenge conventional perceptions of materiality and to reflect on coexistence, dependency and mutual transformation.

Parasite invites the viewer to consider beauty not through purity or separation, but through collision and connection. Elements that appear incompatible can feed into one another, producing new meanings, forms and possibilities.
