SOLO EXHIBITION

Un autre monde dans celui-ci

Sun Yanchu

11 October — 12 November 2025

Exhibition

“There is certainly another world, but it is in this one.” — Paul Éluard

Between photography and painting, there is no clear boundary, but rather a slow-moving mist. It is within this zone of indeterminacy that Sun Yanchu’s Developer Paintings emerge — images awakened by light, chemical baths and time, rising out of darkness only to dissolve into it again. These images are not taken; they are generated. They do not reproduce reality, but result from an encounter between light and matter.

In this solo exhibition, the artist presents his recent Developer Paintings alongside a group of new experimental works. He mounts the developed images onto wooden panels painted with acrylic, creating a fusion between the materiality of photography and that of painting. The fibres of the photographic paper meet the grain of the wood, while accumulated layers give the body of the image a presence that is at once more tangible and more uncertain.

The surfaces of the works retain traces of time and chemical reaction, while the depth of the wood seems to contain the current of another world. The image therefore ceases to be simply a visual surface and becomes a sensitive, breathing space — another world slowly revealed within this one.

Before these works, we seem almost to witness the birth of an image: light moves through darkness, residues accumulate, and forms emerge between erasure and deposition. Every stain and every remnant repeatedly questions the tension between appearance and disappearance.

What is visible? What remains latent? At the very moment an image is revealed, a more intimate and secret world also begins to take shape within it.

Un autre monde dans celui-ci is not simply a poetic phrase, but a way of seeing. Taking matter as his point of entry and time as his medium, Sun Yanchu transforms traces of light into another form of the world — a world that, while already here, continues to reveal itself.
