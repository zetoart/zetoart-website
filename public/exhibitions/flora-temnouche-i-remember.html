SOLO EXHIBITION

I Remember

Flora Temnouche

1 September — 27 September 2023

Exhibition

Flora Temnouche’s practice is centred primarily on painting and on the importance of observational drawing. Her images remain closely connected to immediate experience: everyday objects, places she has passed through, and personal rituals repeatedly enter her work.

These subjects also continue a certain tradition of Western painting, in which familiar motifs and ordinary scenes become vehicles for close observation. Temnouche’s paintings describe intimate and often enclosed spaces, seeking to establish a sense of familiarity between the image and the viewer.

The objects she paints are often commonplace. What interests her is precisely their apparent neutrality: ordinary things can simultaneously seem anonymous and carry a powerful sense of belonging, attachment and personal history.

The writings of Georges Perec are an important influence on Temnouche’s practice. Like Perec, she is interested in how the spirit of a period can emerge through the description of objects and situations that initially seem insignificant.

Through these details, the everyday becomes a form of archive. Objects, interiors and gestures are not simply represented, but become traces of a particular moment, capable of carrying memory and revealing something of the people to whom they belong.

I Remember develops this attention to intimacy, familiarity and memory, transforming fragments of daily life into quiet portraits of lived experience.
