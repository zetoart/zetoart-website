SOLO EXHIBITION

Dans ces OS là !

Jonathan Pêpe

31 August — 21 September 2024

Exhibition

Dans ces OS là ! simulates life and plays with words, their meanings and unconscious resonances through digital networks. These networks become nets through which Jonathan Pêpe connects a corpus of anthropic works exploring relationships between art and science through a series of modular fictions around the Chthulucene.

This fictional near future, developed through the thought of Donna Haraway, allows Pêpe to construct a dialogue between utopia and dystopia through the idea of the prosthesis. The exosomatic object, endowed with behaviour, ceases to remain simply an object and begins to assume the position of a subject.

Dans ces OS là ! brings together sculptures involving fluid systems — digital, petrochemical and gaseous — mechanical systems such as robotic frameworks, both rigid and soft, and programmatic operating systems, or OS.

Through these hybrid structures, the breath of compressors becomes a motor of artificial life. Humans and non-humans coexist within forms of survival, while distinctions between organism, machine, object and environment become increasingly unstable.

Pêpe’s sculptures propose a de-categorisation of evolutionary biology through the idea of Pneuma: the ancient notion of vital breath, reconfigured here through cybernetics and technology. Life is approached not as a fixed category, but as a fragile process of circulation, interaction and equilibrium within a suffering biotope.

Text by Jean-Jacques Gay, 2024
