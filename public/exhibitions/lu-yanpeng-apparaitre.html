SOLO EXHIBITION

APPARAÎTRE

Lu Yanpeng

5 May — 2 June 2026

Exhibition

APPARAÎTRE explores the way images, memories and forms gradually come into presence. In Lu Yanpeng’s photographic practice, the image is not simply captured; it emerges through time, darkness, light and the material process of photography.

Moving between landscape, still life and more intimate fragments, Lu’s photographs often seem suspended between visibility and disappearance. Rather than describing the world directly, they allow it to slowly reveal itself. What appears in the image is therefore never entirely fixed: a landscape may become a memory, an object may become a trace, and darkness itself may become a space from which another image begins to emerge.

The exhibition also includes works from Memory of Stones, a series combining Lu Yanpeng’s photography with paintings on silk by Ling Hua. Created during a period marked by the expectation of new life, the works bring together stone, image and painting, allowing different forms of time and memory to coexist within a single surface.

APPARAÎTRE is ultimately concerned with how the world, life and memory slowly come before us — not as stable images, but as things that continually appear, fade and return.
