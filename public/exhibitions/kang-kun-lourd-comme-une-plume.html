SOLO EXHIBITION

Lourd comme une plume

Kang Kun

27 January — 27 February 2024

Exhibition

Lourd comme une plume is Kang Kun’s first solo exhibition at ZETO ART.

The exhibition introduces a series of contradictions that run through Kang Kun’s recent works: lightness and heaviness, hardness and fragility. These oppositions are embedded within the very texture of the sculptures.

Many of the works appear to possess hard, crystalline shells, sharp points and rough surfaces. Yet on closer inspection, these seemingly rigid structures are formed with unexpectedly soft materials such as wool and feathers.

The apparently solid shells are not indestructible either. Resin and transparent synthetic resin form only thin surfaces that remain vulnerable to physical impact, while materials that seem fragile — wool and feathers — can in fact absorb shock with remarkable resilience.

In French, the expression comme une plume suggests something almost weightless. In Kang Kun’s work, however, this lightness is given an unexpectedly heavy presence. The body appears heavy while the mind remains light as a feather. This tension between body and spirit reflects the artist’s ongoing search for the self.

In recent years, Kang Kun’s sculptures have gradually moved away from distorted human forms toward non-human and animal bodies, and eventually toward hybrid combinations of human and animal forms.

This evolution also reflects a transformation in his understanding of the self. Rather than examining identity as something isolated and internal, Kang Kun increasingly considers the relationship between the individual and the external world, the influence of environment on the self, and the emotional changes produced by familiar and unfamiliar surroundings.

This outward expansion has opened his practice toward increasingly strange and hybrid forms, in which opposing qualities coexist without resolving one another.
