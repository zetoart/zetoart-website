ARTISTS EXHIBITIONS ABOUT CONTACT

SOLO EXHIBITION

Persistance rétinienne

Irène Eon

7 July — 12 August 2023

Exhibition

I recognise nothing in these paintings except things I may once have observed long enough for them to remain attached to my retina. Disembodied forms appear like pure reactions of light at the back of the eye, while the mind struggles to give them meaning.

A glare, the residue of an object contemplated for too long, the incomplete and nebulous memory of a friend’s face: Irène Eon’s paintings seem strangely familiar, as though they had already been seen somewhere before.

Far behind the seat of consciousness, inexplicable figures reappear. Memory, altered by the sensory limits of the body, leaves behind only an eroded image — a fleeting exposure on the film of memory.

What remains of our strongest memories may no longer be the original experience itself, but the fictions constructed after it. In their initial state, memories are only shallow impressions left behind by past emotions: traces whose meaning can no longer be fully deciphered.

What appears on Irène Eon’s canvases is therefore not necessarily our own memories, but a reminder of their fragility. Even the most vivid memories, even those filled with sincere affection, are already dissolving into the past.

Only the present still belongs to us.

Text by Timothée Gruel
