SOLO EXHIBITION

The Breath

Alena Olasyuk

30 April — 28 May 2025

Exhibition

Not all forms of seeing arise from looking. Some begin with breath—with stillness, with descent.

Using a fine nib with ink, the artist draws line after line across the paper. The process becomes a record of time, where each movement leaves behind marks, traces, and occasional drops—accidents that become part of the image.

Repetition here is a rhythm of control followed by release—like the breath itself, with its inhale and exhale. Each line permits deviation. Each gesture carries its own echo. What emerges is not a fixed form, but a structure slowly unfolding—open, irregular, and alive.

At times, black becomes everything. It is not emptiness, but a well—deep, silent, and absorbing. The gaze no longer seeks meaning but rests. Within this darkness, breath finds a place to dwell.

A voice appears, not to explain, but to accompany. The artist’s reading sustains the rhythm begun by the hand. It runs parallel to vision, like breath itself—unspoken, continuous, essential.

These works are not meant to be interpreted, but experienced. They ask not for understanding, but presence. To look, in this space, is to descend—not outward, but inward.
