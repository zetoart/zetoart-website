SOLO EXHIBITION

Le perroquet et son cerisier

Yang Yi

6 September — 30 September 2025

Exhibition

The title of the exhibition, Le perroquet et son cerisier, seems to come straight from a fable, carrying with it a sense of lightness and absurdity. One day, the artist encountered an unexpected scene: a parrot perched in a cherry tree. The image was at once real and improbable, as though reality had suddenly slipped into the realm of a fairy tale.

The encounter became a symbol of those moments that seem slightly out of place — images that resist ordinary logic and, precisely through this dissonance, reveal another way in which the world presents itself to us.

Yang Yi’s paintings seek to capture such moments. Her landscapes do not appear as complete or seamless representations, but are continually disturbed by traces of light and shadow. Light acts like a visitor, cutting through or covering parts of the scene and making the familiar suddenly strange; shadow becomes a subtle annotation, reminding us of the presence of time.

Her paintings may initially appear calm, yet somewhere within them there is always a slight fracture or dissonance that causes the viewer’s gaze to pause.

In this new series, the artist no longer seeks harmony within the landscape, but turns instead toward moments interrupted by time. This approach echoes Bergson’s understanding of duration: real time is not composed of uniform fragments, but unfolds as a continuity traversed by differences, delays and shifts. Every interruption of the instant therefore becomes evidence of the continuity of life itself.

The exhibition is thus not simply a representation of landscape, but an experiment in looking. Like a silent fable, the encounter between the parrot and the cherry tree, and the fractures and interweaving of light and shadow, reveal that a dissonant moment is not a rupture, but the appearance of another form of duration and eternity.
