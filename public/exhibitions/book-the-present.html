GROUP EXHIBITION

Book the Present

Ren Han · Song Xiaoke · Liu Xiaodong · La Maison de Z

8 December 2023 — 13 January 2024

Exhibition

Book the Present is an exhibition about books.

Three artists and an independent publisher are invited to interpret the concept of the book from different perspectives. Ren Han uses the book as a support for creation; in Liu Xiaodong’s paintings, the book becomes a recurring subject; Song Xiaoke uses the book itself as a form of artistic creation; and La Maison de Z presents limited-edition publications in which artists combine their own works and recreate them through the form of the book.

The exhibition considers the book not simply as a container for text or images, but as an object, a medium and a structure through which artistic ideas can be developed and transformed. Each participant approaches its physical and conceptual possibilities in a different way.

December is traditionally a month for giving presents, and books can be among the most meaningful gifts. At the same time, a book can also become a medium through which the “present” — the moment in which we live — is recorded and preserved.

The title Book the Present therefore plays between these two meanings: the book as a gift, and the book as a way of capturing the present.
