SOLO EXHIBITION

Par Nuances

Ren Han

31 October — 28 November 2026

Exhibition

“Omnia mutantur, nihil interit.”
Ovid, Metamorphoses, Book XV

The title Par Nuances echoes Buffon’s reflections in his Natural History on time and the transformations of nature, which unfold by degrees, through subtle differences and in succession. These nuances concern both differences between forms and the ways in which they unsettle established classifications and boundaries.

The exhibition explores the human desire to prolong life, as well as transformations in the forms of living beings and the boundaries of identity. Ren Han draws on images and traditions of knowledge from different periods, including illustrations of the Chinese pharmacopoeia, historical European engravings and natural history plates.

These sources are brought into dialogue with direct observation of plants, animals and bodily forms. They bear witness to human efforts to know, classify and preserve life, while at the same time revealing the limits of our categories and distinctions.

Inscribed on objects associated with care, knowledge and preservation, these images also echo the human desire to care for the body and prolong life.

Through acts of wear, burning, engraving and drawing applied to pre-existing supports — pharmaceutical packaging, old books and other materials — Ren Han brings images back into view within transformed surfaces.

Pre-existing traces and newly produced images overlap, while the original functions and meanings of the objects shift. Historical images are not simply reproduced: they take on another form through contact with new materials and new experiences of looking.

From nuances in vegetal forms to bodies situated between the human and the animal, and images emerging from altered surfaces, the works attend to states that are difficult to delimit with certainty.

Between the persistence and alteration of life, and between the appearance and erasure of forms, subtle differences shift the way we understand living beings and our own identity.

Par Nuances was first presented at ASIA NOW 2026 before continuing at ZETO ART.
