ZETO ART

ZETO ART is a contemporary art gallery founded in 2017 and based in the Marais, Paris.

The gallery develops long-term collaborations with artists from Europe and Asia, presenting practices shaped by distinctive visual languages, material experimentation, and poetic approaches to contemporary art.

Through exhibitions and collaborations, ZETO ART seeks to create encounters between different artistic contexts while supporting the development of emerging and established practices.
