REN Han

Drawing · Sculpture · Installation
China
Biography

REN Han is a Chinese artist whose practice moves between drawing, sculpture and installation.

His work often develops through processes of accumulation, repetition, erosion and transformation, examining the tension between material, image and spatial perception.

Through restrained gestures and carefully constructed surfaces, REN Han creates works in which physical presence and visual ambiguity coexist.
