Yang Yi

Lives and works in Paris

Biography

Yang Yi is a Chinese painter born in Nanjing and currently living and working in Paris. She studied at the Central Academy of Fine Arts in Beijing before graduating from the École Nationale Supérieure des Beaux-Arts de Paris in 2019, where she received her DNSAP with the Félicitations du jury.

Her painting explores the shifting relationship between memory, perception and lived experience. Moving between observed landscapes, everyday scenes and imagined spaces, her works often seem suspended between familiarity and uncertainty. Light plays a recurring role in her practice, appearing both as a subject and as a means of transforming ordinary moments into fleeting and elusive images.
