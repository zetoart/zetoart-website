Flora Temnouche

Lives and works between Paris and Berlin

Biography

Flora Temnouche is a French painter living and working between Paris and Berlin. Her practice is rooted in observation and in the immediate experience of everyday life, drawing on familiar objects, places, rituals and intimate environments.

Her paintings often focus on ordinary subjects whose apparent neutrality carries a sense of familiarity and belonging. Through subtle shifts of light, colour and perspective, Temnouche transforms fragments of daily life into quiet, suspended images, exploring memory, intimacy and the ways in which objects can reflect the character of a particular time.
