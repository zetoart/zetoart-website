Biography

Jonathan Pêpe (b. 1987, Toulouse) is a French artist whose practice moves between drawing, film and installations that may be interactive, evolving or robotic.

Trained at the National School of Art in Bourges and later at Le Fresnoy – Studio national des arts contemporains, he develops fictional forms by diverting contemporary technologies, particularly through experiments with soft robotics.

Often working in dialogue with scientific research laboratories, his practice explores the unstable boundary between the living and the non-living. Through movement, breath, fluid systems and hybrid forms, Pêpe gives apparently inert objects behavioural and organic qualities.
