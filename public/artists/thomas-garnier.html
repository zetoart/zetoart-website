Biography

Thomas Garnier (b. 1991, Paris) is a French contemporary artist whose practice moves between installation, sculpture, moving image and digital technologies.

Originally trained as an architect at ENSA Paris Val de Seine, he later graduated from Le Fresnoy – Studio national des arts contemporains, where his installation Cenotaph received the ADAGP Digital Arts Revelation distinction.

His work explores liminal and heterotopic spaces, questioning the ways architecture, technology and collective imaginaries shape our perception of contemporary environments. Through mechanised installations, 3D-printed structures, projections and image-based works, Garnier constructs fictional landscapes suspended between archaeology, ruin and speculative futures.
