Biography

Huang Xiaoliang (b. 1985, Xiangxi, Hunan) is a Chinese artist working across photography, moving image, painting and multimedia. He graduated from Qingdao University in 2009, where he studied Digital Media.

His practice explores the shifting boundary between reality and fiction through light, shadow, silhouettes and fragments of everyday experience. Ordinary surroundings become theatrical spaces in which memory, perception and chance encounters are reorganised into quietly ambiguous images.

Moving between the positions of observer and participant, Huang approaches daily life as an unfolding stage. His works often combine photographic perception with a painterly sensibility, transforming fleeting moments into images suspended between recollection, imagination and lived reality.
