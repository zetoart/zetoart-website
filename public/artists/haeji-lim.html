Haeji Lim

Lives and works in Seoul

Biography

Haeji Lim is a Korean artist born in Seoul in 1988. She graduated from the École Nationale Supérieure des Beaux-Arts de Paris in 2017 and currently lives and works in Seoul.

Her practice explores states of unease, intimacy and emotional uncertainty through painting. In her earlier works, solitary figures, architectural spaces and familiar objects appear within strangely suspended environments. Recognisable scenes are often transformed through unusual colours, empty spaces and unsettling spatial relationships, creating a tension between what feels intimate and what remains difficult to grasp.

In her more recent works, this tension increasingly moves away from figurative representation toward monochromatic and more abstract images. Working with oil pastel, Lim explores experiences of insomnia, anxiety and unresolved emotions, allowing light and darkness, repetition and blurred forms to coexist on the surface. Across these different bodies of work, painting becomes a way of leaving traces of emotional experience and giving form to states that remain unstable, ambiguous and deeply personal.
