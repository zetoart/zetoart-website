Kang Kun

Biography

Kang Kun is a Korean artist whose practice develops around sculpture and installation. He studied at the European School of Art of Brittany in Rennes, where he obtained his DNAP in 2015, and graduated with a DNSEP from the École Nationale Supérieure d'Arts de Paris-Cergy in 2017.

Working with resin, thread, wool, fabric, faux fur and needles, Kang approaches sculpture through a tension between softness and resistance. Human bodies, fragmented figures, doubles and hybrid forms repeatedly appear in his work, while fragile materials are assembled into structures that seem at once vulnerable and strangely resilient.

Questions of identity and relationships with others run throughout his practice. Figures are duplicated, connected or transformed into twins and clones, evoking the shifting image of the self formed through memory, social expectations and the gaze of others. Needles and threads function simultaneously as physical connections and as traces of tension, dependency and vulnerability within these relationships.
