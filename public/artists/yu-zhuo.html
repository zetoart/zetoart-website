YU Zhuo

Biography

YU Zhuo was born in Wuhan, China, in 1990 and currently lives and works in China. After graduating in Visual Arts from the Macao Polytechnic Institute in 2012, YU continued studies in France between 2013 and 2017, including photography at EFET in Paris.

YU Zhuo’s photographic practice is closely connected to the experience of night. Darkness appears not simply as a setting, but as a psychological and emotional space — mysterious, distant and capable of containing both strength and fragility. Through sustained observation of nocturnal spaces, YU explores the relationship between the individual, solitude and the meanings projected onto the world around us.

The night may offer a form of refuge, yet YU’s work also questions the stability of that refuge. What happens when the objects, spaces or experiences in which we place our emotional dependence suddenly lose their meaning? The photographs move between shelter and uncertainty, inviting the viewer to reconsider darkness as both an intimate territory and a space of unresolved possibility.
