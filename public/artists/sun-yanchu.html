Sun Yanchu

Lives and works in Zhengzhou, China

Biography

Sun Yanchu is a Chinese artist born in Zhoukou, Henan Province, in 1978. He currently lives and works in Zhengzhou.

His practice moves between photography and painting, centred on a process he calls Developer Paintings. Working in the darkroom without a camera or film, Sun paints directly with developer and fixer on unexposed photographic material. The image gradually emerges through chemical reaction, light and time, introducing an element of unpredictability into the act of image-making.

In his recent works, these developed images are mounted onto aluminium, wood or other supports and combined with acrylic, watercolor, oil and other painterly materials. Photography becomes not a means of recording the visible world, but a material process through which images appear, dissolve and transform. His landscapes, trees, stars and fragments of light occupy an uncertain space between representation and abstraction, presence and disappearance.
