ARTISTS ABOUT CONTACT

Shen Wei

Lives and works in New York City

Biography

Shen Wei is a Chinese-born American artist based in New York City. Working across photography and painting, his practice explores identity, memory, desire and the relationship between the body and its surrounding environment.

He is known for his intimate portraits and self-portraits, as well as his poetic photographs of landscapes and plant life. Moving between introspection and theatricality, his photographic work often places the body within charged or dreamlike spaces, while nature appears as a reflection of memory, melancholy and inner experience.

In his recent paintings, Shen extends the narrative of his photographic self-portraits onto the canvas. Nature becomes both metaphor and muse, allowing memory and fantasy, intimacy and longing, the real and the imagined to overlap. Across photography and painting, his work constructs an intimate visual world in which the body, plants and everyday surroundings continuously transform one another.
